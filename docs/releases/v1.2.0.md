Browse saves in a calmer single-column feed or compact grid, organize them directly on each card, and refresh just the accounts or Instagram collections you need.

- **Feed and grid:** animated layout switching keeps the post you are reading visible. Instagram feed previews are smaller, X previews use a more compact scale, and grid cards have subtly tinted action and collection bars.
- **Notes and collections:** edit autosaved sticky notes beside feed posts, expand long notes, and add or create collections from each card. Browse Accounts or Collections in the sidebar, with collapsible Added today and Added earlier sections.
- **Better previews:** saved metadata stays visible while embeds load. Visible posts take loading priority, confirmed failures retry automatically, and each post has a manual Retry button.
- **Targeted, faster refresh:** quick scans stop after seven consecutive familiar posts, resetting the count whenever a new post appears. Refresh a single account or Instagram collection, or choose Quick scan or Deep scan for all sources. Customize which Instagram collections appear in your library.
- **Background browser work:** temporary helper tabs stay inactive, muted, and grouped as Save Extension Tabs, then close after completion, cancellation, or failure.
- **Unsave with confirmation:** explicitly unsave an X or Instagram post from its source account. Local deletion follows verification; shared saves remain for other accounts. Archive remains a separate, reversible way to exclude a save from normal browsing and MCP.
- **Exact X account order:** individual X accounts follow their observed bookmark-list order, including existing bookmarks moved to the top.
- **Simpler AI connection:** one Copy connection prompt button works with MCP-compatible local AI apps. It includes the running app’s address, preserves model/provider settings, and asks the client to verify access without changing saves. Copy post references to point your AI at a specific save.
- **Less interface clutter:** sticky search and view controls, keyboard search and Escape restoration, compact date separators, and copy guidance shown only in the toast.

Existing saves, notes, topics, archives, and source collection memberships are preserved. X and Instagram do not reliably expose the original saved date; Added today and Added earlier use the date a post entered saveXtension.

**Browser helper update:** version 1.8.4 is included. Reload saveXtension once in your browser’s Extensions page after updating. If upgrading from a helper older than 1.8.3, approve tab-group access if the browser asks. Quick scans check new saves near the top; use a manual Deep scan for older gaps.

Update through Settings → App updates, or quit saveXtension and install the matching download: DMG for Apple Silicon Macs, EXE for Windows x64. On Mac, replace the app in Applications and reopen it.

Signing is unchanged: the Mac app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; the Windows installer has no publisher signature. Installers publish after both native builds and their desktop checks pass.
